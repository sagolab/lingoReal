Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lingo Real ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile applications including SpeakSwipe, Verbally AI, Lingo Real – AI Language Lessons, and other products (collectively, the "Apps").

App-specific details: Lingo Real – AI Language Lessons.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Account information (email address, display name) if you create an account
  • Audio recordings when you use speech practice features (processed in real-time and not stored permanently)
  • Language preferences and learning progress

Information collected automatically:

  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • App usage data (features used, session duration, crash logs)
  • Analytics data through third-party services (Firebase Analytics)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Apps' functionality
  • To process your speech for pronunciation scoring
  • To track your learning progress and streaks
  • To send you relevant notifications (if opted in)
  • To analyze app performance and fix bugs

3. Speech and Audio Data

When you use speech practice features, your audio is processed in real-time to provide pronunciation feedback. Audio recordings are processed by our speech recognition partners (including Google and Deepgram) and are not permanently stored on our servers. We do not sell or share your audio data with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Apps (cloud hosting, analytics, speech processing)
  • Law enforcement if required by law

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Learning progress is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud). We retain your data for as long as you maintain an account with us.

6. Your Rights

You may:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Disable microphone access at any time through your device settings

7. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Third-Party Services

Our Apps may contain links to third-party services (such as YouTube videos). These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy in the App or on our website.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

Email: support@lingoreal.com
Website: https://lingoreal.com
